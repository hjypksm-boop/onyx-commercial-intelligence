# 5-minute interview walkthrough

Sign in as `cs.exec@demo.local` / `Demo123!`. Tip: the sidebar **Start tour** button walks the same path.

**0:00 — Frame it (Executive Overview).** "This is a synthetic CS platform. Three questions on one screen." Point at the
banner, then the four KPI cards: ARR £270,000, adoption 70.4% (50 of 71 seats), revenue at risk £96,000, potential
expansion £62,000. Open one **Show SQL** — every number is a SQL view over stored records.

**0:45 — Who is getting value?** Northstar, Atlantic, Harbour, Summit — each with a data-backed reason. Click
**Northstar** → Customer 360: 85% seat adoption, MAU rising for a year, every analytics feature adopted, heavy API use.

**1:30 — Who is at risk?** Back to the overview → **Gulf Marine Fuels** (top of *Accounts requiring immediate
attention*). Customer 360 shows the MAU chart and the computed warning *"Active users declined 60% from June to
November (10 → 4)"*. Health tab: score ~40 with the component breakdown — usage trend and adoption drive it. Support
tab: an escalated High ticket that breached SLA. Overview tab: the champion left in August.

**2:30 — Act on it (Health & Retention).** Open the recovery plan. Step 1 surfaces evidence *and* alternative
explanations ("don't assume low usage means dissatisfaction"). Step 2: click **Suggest hypotheses** — saved only as
*Hypothesis* until confirmed against a recorded customer conversation. Step 4 creates owned, dated tasks that appear
instantly in Tasks & Follow-ups.

**3:30 — Where can we grow? (Growth Opportunities).** Potential £62,000 vs probability-weighted £30,200 — shown
separately. Move *Additional analytics seats* to Negotiation; the stage history records it. Mention Closed Won moves
value to *booked new ACV*.

**4:15 — Report it.** Switch Demo Mode to **Scenario C** (sandbox — baseline untouched). Commercial Close Report:
8 meetings, 3 new opportunities worth £75,000 potential pipeline (amber, *not booked*), 2 accounts at risk.
Draft commentary, edit, **Export PDF**.

**4:45 — Close.** Settings → change a health weight: the live preview shows category changes before saving, and the
weights must total 100%. Every change is in the audit trail.
